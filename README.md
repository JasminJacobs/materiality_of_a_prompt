# Materiality of a Prompt

How many kilometres of cable, of what material, does one prompt need from your device to the answer and back?

Prototype for the MakeSense Workshop, ÉCAL, November 2026. Three map scenes: dive into your 250 m square, the route through Switzerland to the hub, and the possible data centres abroad.

Solid white = measured or observed. Blue dashed = estimated. Pink = nothing is published.

## Data
- BAKOM/OFCOM broadband atlas, 250 m grid (fibre, coax, copper), `grid.json`
- Coastlines: Natural Earth (public domain), `land.json`
- Epoch AI, AI data centers (CC BY 4.0)
- Cable and antenna weights from manufacturer datasheets; full source list on the page
- Own traceroutes from ÉCAL Wi-Fi and Sunrise mobile, 17 Nov 2026
